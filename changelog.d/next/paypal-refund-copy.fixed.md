PayPal refunds, reversals and held refund notices now read in plain words. Activity shows "Refund recorded" and "Disputed payment restored" instead of an "Unrecognized marketplace event" warning, and a refund row keeps its title when the order is later reversed or refunded again. A refunded order says "Refunded" or "Payment reversed" instead of "refunded external", gives the refund amount and reference, and explains a reversal, a cancelled reversal, or a PayPal refund notice that is on hold or could not be applied automatically. If PayPal refunds or reverses an order while you act on it, the message says so, and a delivery confirmation is no longer reported as done when PayPal refunded the order first. A restored payment badges only when it hands the order back to you.
