Bitcoin checkout says "Sent to your wallet" instead of "Delivered to your wallet", because the Shop can't see the wallet accept the request, and suggests checking that the seller is a Bitkit contact if the request is missing. A paid Bitcoin order shows "Seller confirmed payment" separately from the on-chain state: "Confirmed on-chain" appears only once the marketplace recorded a confirmation, and otherwise the card says the seller confirmed before it saw one. The seller's confirm panel shows the confirm-by time as a readable date with a live countdown, and the observed time as a readable date, instead of raw timestamps.
