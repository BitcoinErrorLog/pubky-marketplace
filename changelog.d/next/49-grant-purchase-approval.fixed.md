Approve purchases now always shows a grant link that Bitkit and Pubky Ring 1.19 or later can approve, whichever way you signed in. An account created by scanning the Pubky Ring create QR, including with Bitkit, used to get the older Pubky Ring link, which Bitkit rejects, so the approval waited and then said it had expired. The dialog and the listing card now say "Approve purchases in Pubky Ring or Bitkit" for those accounts, and if an approval lapses the dialog says which apps can approve it.
