Create account shows one sign-up QR that Pubky Ring or Bitkit can scan to create a new pubky, with a link to Sign in for anyone whose pubky is already in Pubky Ring or Bitkit. The separate Bitkit sign-up QR is gone, because Bitkit treats it as a sign-in for a pubky it already holds and creates no account. The step header now reads "Create your pubky" instead of "Use Pubky Ring", and the "scan the QR again" hint is removed.
