Buyers open digital purchases from the order: a file is fetched from the seller's homeserver, checked against what was paid for, decrypted on the device and saved; a link or text is shown on request until hidden. An emailed item shows where the seller will send it, with Change until it is marked emailed, and asks for an address if none is on file. Digital orders read "Delivered · ready to download", offer no return, and sellers see no shipping actions on them.
