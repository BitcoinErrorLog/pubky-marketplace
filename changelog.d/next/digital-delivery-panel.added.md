Sellers set how buyers receive a published digital listing from its edit page: a file encrypted in the browser and stored on the seller's homeserver, a link, a text, an email sent by the seller, or a message sent by the seller. The panel shows what buyers receive now, which version is live and how many buyers still download an earlier version, and it can replace or remove the delivery. Files over the deployment's size cap, links that are not https, and a full homeserver are refused with plain copy.
