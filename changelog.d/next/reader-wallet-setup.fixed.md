When you pay with Bitcoin before your Paykit wallet has finished setting up, the Shop now says "Reader wallet setup needed: finish setting up Bitkit (or another Paykit wallet) for this pubky, then try again", with a Try again button, instead of a generic refusal. Nothing retries on its own; Try again runs Pay again only when you choose it. This follows the marketplace service's new refusal for that case (pubky/pubky-marketplace-service#84, pubky/paykit-server#45).
