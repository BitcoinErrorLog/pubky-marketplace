Sellers deliver emailed and message-kind digital items from the order: a paid order waiting on them reads "To deliver", Show email reveals the buyer's address with how to use it, and Mark emailed or Mark delivered completes delivery. The address leaves the page as soon as the order is cancelled, refunded or closed. Every paid digital order shows its delivery evidence, such as "Delivered automatically · first opened Sep 26, 14:02 · opened 3 times" or "Marked emailed Sep 26, 14:05"; if that record can't be loaded, the order says so and offers Retry. Sellers can record a refund on a delivered or completed digital order, and approving a cancel says opened files stay counted as sold.
