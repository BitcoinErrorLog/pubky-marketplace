The Shop can run mounted under a path on another origin (for example `https://pubky.app/shop`) and inside an iframe there. New settings, all off by default: `NEXT_PUBLIC_BASE_PATH` and `NEXT_PUBLIC_ASSET_PREFIX` (build), `PUBKY_RUNTIME_FRAME_ANCESTORS`, `PUBKY_RUNTIME_EMBEDDED` and `PUBKY_RUNTIME_STORAGE_ADOPT_LEGACY` (runtime). When embedded, the Shop hides its own header, footer and floating button, registers no service worker, and the PayPal checkout hand-off leaves the frame through the top window. See `docs/launch/shop-embedding.md`.
